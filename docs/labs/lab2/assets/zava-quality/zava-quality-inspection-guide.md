# ZavaCore Quality Inspection Guide

This synthetic guide exists only for the PPCC ALM workshop. It is not a real Zava or Microsoft manufacturing policy.

## Purpose

Use this guide to classify and escalate quality issues found during inspection of ZavaCore products.

## Required incident information

Record:

- Batch ID
- ZavaCore product
- Date and time observed
- Short description of the issue
- Severity
- Current status
- Assigned owner

Do not include customer personal data, employee personal data, credentials, or secrets.

## Severity 1: Observation

Use **Observation** when:

- The issue is cosmetic.
- Product performance and safety are unaffected.
- Packaging or labeling has a minor defect but remains readable and traceable.

Action:

1. Record the observation.
2. Continue inspection.
3. Assign an owner before the end of the shift.

## Severity 2: Quality hold

Use **Quality hold** when:

- A required measurement is outside its approved target.
- A batch record or traceability record is missing.
- Packaging or labeling could cause the wrong product or batch to be selected.
- The issue could affect product performance but there is no immediate safety indication.

Action:

1. Stop movement of the affected batch.
2. Label and isolate the batch.
3. Record the batch ID and evidence.
4. Notify the quality lead within four business hours.
5. Do not release the batch until the quality lead records a disposition.

## Severity 3: Critical

Use **Critical** when:

- A safety check fails.
- Contamination is suspected.
- A sensor or powered component shows overheating, exposed conductors, smoke, or unexpected energy discharge.
- Product identity or traceability cannot be established for material that has already left the controlled area.

Action:

1. Stop work immediately.
2. Keep people away from the affected product or equipment.
3. Isolate the batch without handling a suspected electrical or contamination hazard.
4. Notify the quality lead and site safety contact immediately.
5. Follow site emergency procedures when there is an active hazard.

## Closing an incident

Only the assigned quality owner can mark an incident **Closed**. The record must include:

- Investigation summary
- Disposition
- Corrective action, when required
- Approval date
- Approver

## Questions outside this guide

This guide does not define:

- Customer discounts or refunds
- Warranty terms
- Regulatory conclusions
- Legal advice
- Product-design changes
- Final safety clearance

Escalate those questions to the quality lead or the responsible business owner.
